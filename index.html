About Me

I'm in my first semester of Computer Science at Vedam School of Technology. Before college started I spent the summer as a Python intern at NXtutors, an education company in Gurugram, working on their live websites. I also run three online businesses of my own: CalmNCalculated, ThreadsWellThought and Khurafatee, all live and taking orders.

I'm fast with AI coding tools, and I run every project from its own knowledge base: a structured set of notes, decisions and checklists that keeps the work organised and easy to pick up again. What I'd call my real strength is checking the work: backing things up before changing them, testing a change before it goes live, and making sure something actually worked instead of trusting a tool that says it did.

At Vedam I'm a member of the Tech Club, the Entrepreneurship Club and the Cultural Club.
